# Google Play 开发者账号注册清单：从付费到身份验证

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[Google Play 开发者账号注册清单：从付费到身份验证](https://fatevia.cn/blog/articles/google-play-console-signup-checklist.html)

按官方路径整理 Play Console 注册步骤、一次性费用与身份验证准备要点，适合首次开通 Google 开发者账号的团队。

## 注册前确认

确认你已年满官方要求年龄，并准备好可用于支付注册费的银行卡。个人用途与组织用途决定账号类型，建议先阅读 Google 关于账号类型的说明。

## 注册费与协议

Play 管理中心通常收取一次性注册费（常见为 25 美元）。注册过程中需要接受开发者分发协议，费用与支付方式请以官方页面为准。

## 身份验证材料

Google 要求完成开发者身份验证。个人与组织材料不同，组织场景可能涉及邓白氏编码等主体信息。材料需真实、一致，避免昵称或错误主体名称。

## 上架前常见卡点

首次发布可能还涉及测试与设备相关要求。建议把官方帮助中心文档加入收藏，政策变化时及时复核，而不是依赖二手传闻。

# Google Play Console signup checklist: fee to verification

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [Google Play Console signup checklist: fee to verification](https://fatevia.cn/en/blog/articles/google-play-console-signup-checklist.html)

A practical checklist for Play Console enrollment, registration fee, and identity verification prep.

## Before you start

Confirm age requirements and a supported payment method. Decide whether a personal or organization account fits your use case.

## Fee and agreement

Google typically charges a one-time registration fee. Review and accept the Developer Distribution Agreement during signup.

## Identity verification

Prepare accurate legal identity details. Organization accounts may require additional business identifiers such as a D-U-N-S number.

## Pre-publish checks

First releases may include testing requirements. Prefer official Help Center articles over unverified shortcuts.
