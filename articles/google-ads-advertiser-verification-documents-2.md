# Google Ads 广告客户验证怎么过：材料准备与验证失败后的账户处理

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[Google Ads 广告客户验证怎么过：材料准备与验证失败后的账户处理](https://fatevia.cn/blog/articles/google-ads-advertiser-verification-documents-2.html)

Google Ads 广告客户验证本质是核对身份、业务与付款方是否一致。本文说明个人与企业该准备哪些材料、常见不通过的原因，以及逾期或失败后账户会受到什么影响。

## 验证不是考试，核心是三项信息能互相印证

广告客户验证要核对的是「谁在投放、谁在付款、业务是否真实存在」。系统和审核人员看的不是材料数量，而是账户资料、证件或企业文件、付款资料、网站公开信息之间能否对得上。所以与其问怎么过，不如先问自己的资料是否一致。绝大多数卡住的情况，都出在名称、地址或主体不一致，而不是材料不够多。具体要求会随国家、地区和账户类型变化，以账户内验证页面和邮件通知为准。

## 先在后台确认自己被要求验证什么

验证入口通常在账户的账单或管理相关菜单下，名称为广告客户验证。进入后先看三件事：系统要求的是身份验证、业务运营验证，还是两者都要；截止时间是否在页面或通知中给出；账户的广告主类型是个人还是组织。这三项决定后面的材料清单。不要凭网上别人的清单提前准备，因为不同账户被要求的项目可能不同。若页面没有出现验证任务，说明当前账户未被要求，不需要主动寻找替代入口。

## 个人与组织的材料清单不同

以个人身份投放时，一般需要真实姓名、所在国家或地区、政府签发的有效身份证件，以及与付款资料一致的姓名。以组织身份投放时，一般需要法定公司名称、注册地址、营业执照或同类登记文件、公司官网、联系电话与邮箱，部分情况还会要求税号或能证明经营地址的文件。如果你是代理商替客户投放，需要说明广告主与付款方的关系，并按页面要求提供授权或客户主体的资料。所有文件要求清晰、完整、未过期、未遮挡关键字段。具体接受哪些文件类型，以页面列出的为准。

## 最常见的不通过原因是名称与付款人不一致

典型冲突有四类。一是付款资料里的名称与证件或营业执照不一致，例如用个人信用卡支付企业广告，或用法人名下的卡但主体填成公司。二是账户填写的公司名是品牌名而非注册全称。三是官网没有任何公司名称、地址或联系方式，无法与提交文件互相印证。四是文件有效期、扫描质量或裁剪问题导致字段无法辨认。提交前逐项比对：账户主体、付款资料名称、文件名称、网站展示信息，是否为同一主体及同一地址。若实际付款方不是广告主，应在验证中如实说明，而不是改名称去凑一致。

## 验证失败或逾期，账户会发生什么

一般的结果是分阶段的：先收到要求完成验证的通知并带有截止期限；逾期未完成，广告可能被限制展示；继续不处理，账户可能被暂停。提交后被拒绝时，通常会给出原因类别，可以在更正后重新提交。具体期限、限制范围和审核时长由平台当时的页面和通知决定，不能假设固定天数。判断标准很简单：只要通知里有截止时间，就按它倒推，至少留出一次补材料的余量。

## 同一原因连续失败两次就该停下来核对，不要换账户绕过

如果同一原因被拒两次，说明问题不在材料清晰度，而在主体关系，比如公司名与付款人始终对不上，或网站无法证明业务存在。此时应先修正根本问题：更换为正确主体的付款方式、补全官网公司信息，或改用实际经营主体开户。不要为了避开验证而新开账户重复提交，平台政策把规避系统视为独立违规，可能导致关联账户一并受限。如果确认资料无误仍被拒，可通过账户内的申诉或支持渠道说明，而不是反复提交相同文件。

# Google Ads Advertiser Verification: What to Prepare and What Happens If It Fails

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [Google Ads Advertiser Verification: What to Prepare and What Happens If It Fails](https://fatevia.cn/en/blog/articles/google-ads-advertiser-verification-documents-2.html)

Google Ads advertiser verification checks that identity, business and payer details match. Here is what individuals and companies typically prepare, why submissions get rejected, and how accounts are affected.

## Verification is a consistency check, not an exam

Advertiser verification asks who is advertising, who is paying, and whether the business actually exists. Reviewers care less about how many documents you upload than whether the account profile, ID or business records, payment profile and public website agree with each other. Most stalled submissions come from mismatched names or addresses, not missing paperwork. Requirements vary by country and account type, so the verification page and notification emails in your account are the source of truth.

## Confirm what your account is actually asked to verify

The task usually sits under the billing or admin area as advertiser verification. Check three things first: whether you need identity verification, business operations verification, or both; whether a deadline is shown; and whether the account is set up as an individual or an organization. Those answers drive your document list. Do not prepare from a checklist you found online, since different accounts can be asked for different items. If no task appears, your account has not been asked to verify and there is nothing to hunt for.

## Individuals and organizations submit different documents

Individual advertisers typically need a legal name, country or region, a valid government-issued ID, and a payment profile name that matches. Organizations typically need the registered legal name, registered address, a business registration document or equivalent, a company website, and contact details; tax IDs or proof of operating address may be requested in some cases. Agencies running ads for clients should be ready to explain the relationship between advertiser and payer and to supply the authorization or client entity details the page asks for. Files should be sharp, complete, unexpired and uncropped. Accepted document types are listed on the verification page itself.

## The usual cause of rejection is a name that does not match the payer

Four patterns recur. The payment profile name differs from the ID or business license, for example a personal card paying for a company account. The account uses a brand name instead of the registered legal name. The website shows no company name, address or contact information that corroborates the documents. Or a file is expired, blurry or cropped so key fields are unreadable. Before submitting, compare the account entity, payment profile name, document name and website details: they should point to the same entity and address. If the real payer is not the advertiser, say so in the process instead of editing names to force a match.

## What happens when verification fails or lapses

Consequences are generally staged. You receive a notice with a deadline; if it passes, ad serving may be restricted; if the issue continues, the account can be suspended. A rejection normally comes with a reason category, and you can correct the data and resubmit. Exact deadlines, restrictions and review times depend on the platform's pages and notices at the time, so do not assume fixed numbers. A practical rule: work backwards from any stated deadline and leave room for at least one round of corrections.

## Two rejections for the same reason means stop and fix the root issue

If the same reason appears twice, document quality is not the problem; the entity relationship is. Typical fixes are using a payment method held by the correct entity, adding company details to the website, or opening the account under the entity that actually operates the business. Do not open new accounts to dodge verification: circumventing systems is a separate policy violation and can cause related accounts to be limited. If your data is correct and you are still rejected, use the in-account appeal or support channel to explain, rather than resubmitting identical files.
