# 新加坡公司主体 Google Play 开发者账号：资料准备与下单核对清单

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[新加坡公司主体 Google Play 开发者账号：资料准备与下单核对清单](https://faya101.com/blog/articles/singapore-company-google-play-developer-account-checklist-2.html)

办理新加坡公司主体的谷歌开发者账号，需准备 ACRA 商业登记文件、董事身份证明及本地支付通道。本文详细梳理注册与购买现号时的资料核对标准，明确主体一致性与支付风控边界，帮助开发者规避审核风险。

## 基础资料清单与主体资质要求

注册或购买新加坡公司主体的 Google Play 开发者账号，核心资料包括新加坡会计与企业管制局（ACRA）出具的最新 BizFile（商业登记文件）、公司董事的有效护照或身份证，以及新加坡本地手机号码。BizFile 必须显示公司状态为「Live」且包含完整的 UEN（唯一实体编号）。若用于购买现号，需确保卖家提供的 ACRA 文件与 Google Play Console 中填写的商家资料完全一致，避免主体信息冲突导致审核驳回。

## 支付通道与商家资料的一致性核对

Google Play 开发者账号的 25 美元注册费及后续的应用内购抽成结算，高度依赖支付通道的合规性。新加坡公司主体必须使用与公司名称一致的万事达或 Visa 信用卡支付注册费，且结算账户需为新加坡本地对公银行账户。在下单购买现号前，必须核对账号内的 Payments Profile（付款资料）是否已完成新加坡主体的税务信息（如 GST 注册号，若适用）和银行账户验证。若付款资料主体与 ACRA 文件不符，账号将面临极高的封禁风险。

## 购买现号时的权限交接与安全核验

若选择直接购买已注册的新加坡公司老号，资料核对的重心需从注册文件转移到账号控制权。下单前需确认卖家移交的 Google 账号已绑定买家的恢复邮箱和手机号，且 2FA（双因素认证）设备已完成解绑或物理移交。同时，需在 Play Console 后台核对「用户和权限」设置，确保原管理员权限已移除，新管理员拥有完整的「管理账号详细信息」和「管理付款资料」权限，防止后续因权限缺失导致无法修改结算信息。

## 适用场景与区域风控边界

新加坡公司主体适合面向东南亚及全球市场发行应用，其企业信誉度在 Google 审核中具有一定优势。但需注意，Google 对东南亚地区账号的登录环境风控较为严格。购买或使用新加坡主体账号时，应确保登录 IP 地址、设备语言及网络环境与账号注册地或目标发行地保持稳定匹配。若频繁在异地 IP 登录且未做环境隔离，极易触发 Google 的异常活动检测，导致账号被限制或封禁。

# Singapore Entity Google Play Developer Account: Document Checklist and Verification

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [Singapore Entity Google Play Developer Account: Document Checklist and Verification](https://faya101.com/en/blog/articles/singapore-company-google-play-developer-account-checklist-2.html)

Registering or purchasing a Singapore entity Google Play developer account requires ACRA BizFile, director ID, and local payment channels. This guide outlines document verification standards for registration and purchasing existing accounts, clarifying entity consistency and payment risk boundaries.

## Core Document Checklist and Entity Requirements

To register or purchase a Google Play developer account under a Singapore entity, the essential documents include the latest BizFile issued by the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA), valid passports or IDs of the company directors, and a local Singapore mobile number. The BizFile must show the company status as Live and include the complete UEN (Unique Entity Number). When purchasing an existing account, ensure the ACRA documents provided by the seller exactly match the merchant details filled in the Google Play Console to avoid rejection due to entity conflicts.

## Aligning Payment Channels with Merchant Profiles

The $25 registration fee and subsequent in-app purchase payout settlements for a Google Play developer account rely heavily on payment channel compliance. A Singapore entity must use a Mastercard or Visa credit card matching the company name for the registration fee, and the settlement account must be a local Singapore corporate bank account. Before purchasing an existing account, verify whether the Payments Profile has completed tax information (e.g., GST registration number, if applicable) and bank account verification for the Singapore entity. Mismatches between the payment profile entity and ACRA documents pose a high risk of account suspension.

## Access Handover and Security Verification for Purchased Accounts

If you choose to purchase an already registered Singapore entity account, the focus of document verification shifts from registration files to account control. Before placing an order, confirm that the transferred Google account is linked to the buyer's recovery email and phone number, and that 2FA devices have been unbound or physically handed over. Additionally, check the Users and permissions settings in the Play Console to ensure the original admin rights are removed, and the new admin has full Manage account details and Manage payment profiles permissions to prevent future settlement issues.

## Use Cases and Regional Risk Control Boundaries

A Singapore entity is suitable for distributing apps in Southeast Asia and globally, offering a credibility advantage in Google's review process. However, Google enforces strict login environment risk controls for accounts in this region. When using or purchasing a Singapore entity account, ensure that the login IP address, device language, and network environment remain stable and match the account's registration or target distribution region. Frequent logins from different regional IPs without proper environment isolation can easily trigger Google's unusual activity detection, leading to account restrictions or bans.
