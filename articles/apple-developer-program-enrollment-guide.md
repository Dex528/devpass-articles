# 苹果开发者账号怎么注册：个人与组织路径说明

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[苹果开发者账号怎么注册：个人与组织路径说明](https://fatevia.cn/blog/articles/apple-developer-program-enrollment-guide.html)

梳理 Apple Developer Program 注册流程、年费概念与个人/组织差异，帮助你准备苹果开发者账号材料。

## 先明确会员目标

若只需学习与设备测试，免费 Apple 开发者资源可能已够用；若要上架 App Store，需要加入 Apple Developer Program。

## 个人路径要点

使用启用双重认证的 Apple 账户，并填写法定姓名。错误使用昵称或公司名填入姓名栏，常导致审批延误。

## 组织路径要点

组织需为可签约的法人实体，通常需要 D-U-N-S 编号，并以组织法定名称显示开发者身份。

## 年费提醒

会员年费常见为 99 美元，地区货币显示可能不同。请以 Apple 官方结账信息为准。

# How to enroll in the Apple Developer Program

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [How to enroll in the Apple Developer Program](https://fatevia.cn/en/blog/articles/apple-developer-program-enrollment-guide.html)

Individual and organization enrollment notes for an Apple Developer account.

## Membership goal

Publishing on the App Store requires Apple Developer Program membership, not only a free Apple ID developer access level.

## Individual path

Use an Apple Account with two-factor authentication and your legal name. Nicknames delay review.

## Organization path

You typically need a legal entity and a D-U-N-S number. The organization name becomes the seller name.

## Fees

Membership is commonly US$99 per year. Confirm local pricing on Apple’s site.
