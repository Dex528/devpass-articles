# 苹果开发者账号年费与会员权益速览

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[苹果开发者账号年费与会员权益速览](https://fatevia.cn/blog/articles/apple-developer-program-fee-and-benefits.html)

用可读方式概括 Apple Developer Program 年费概念与上架相关权益，方便预算评估。

## 年费是什么

加入 Apple Developer Program 通常按年付费。具体金额与币种以 Apple 官方展示为准。

## 会员能做什么

会员可使用完整分发能力、TestFlight、App Store Connect 等工具，具体权益见官方对比页。

## 免费账户差异

免费开发者访问可覆盖学习与部分测试，但不足以完成正式 App Store 分发。

## 预算建议

把年费、主体注册（如需）、以及可能的顾问服务费分开记账，避免混为一谈。

## Annual membership

Program membership is billed annually. Confirm the live price in your region.

## What membership unlocks

Distribution tooling, TestFlight, and App Store Connect capabilities are part of membership.

## Free access limits

Free developer resources help learning and some testing, not full App Store distribution.

## Budgeting

Separate platform fees from entity setup and optional consulting costs.
