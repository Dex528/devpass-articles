# 苹果开发者账号 D-U-N-S 编号：组织注册前必看

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[苹果开发者账号 D-U-N-S 编号：组织注册前必看](https://fatevia.cn/blog/articles/apple-developer-duns-number-basics.html)

解释苹果公司开发者账号常见的 D-U-N-S 要求、申请与同步注意点，降低组织注册卡单概率。

## 为什么需要 D-U-N-S

Apple 用 D-U-N-S 核验组织法人实体身份。个人会员通常不需要；公司和教育机构组织注册通常需要。

## 申请与同步

可先在 D&B 查询是否已有编号；新申请后，信息同步到 Apple 可能需要一定工作日，请预留时间。

## 名称必须一致

提交给 Apple 的组织名称应与法人实体及 D&B 资料一致。商号、分公司名称等常见不被接受为组织会员主体。

## 实用建议

把营业执照类文件、授权联系人和邮箱提前备齐，可减少来回补充材料的时间。

# D-U-N-S basics for Apple Developer organization enrollment

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [D-U-N-S basics for Apple Developer organization enrollment](https://fatevia.cn/en/blog/articles/apple-developer-duns-number-basics.html)

What a D-U-N-S number is for, and how to avoid common organization enrollment delays.

## Why Apple asks for it

Apple uses D-U-N-S to verify legal entity status for organization membership.

## Timing

After obtaining a number, allow time for D&B data to sync to Apple before enrolling.

## Name consistency

Use the legal entity name consistently across D&B and Apple. Trade names are commonly rejected.

## Prep tip

Prepare authorization contacts and entity documents before you start the enrollment form.
