# 加拿大苹果开发者账号申请与App上架资料清单

本文件是网站文章的副本。正式页面：[加拿大苹果开发者账号申请与App上架资料清单](https://faya101.com/blog/articles/canada-apple-developer-account-app-listing-materials-2.html)

获取加拿大苹果开发者账号并上架App，需明确主体资质与资料清单。本文梳理个人与公司号的资质要求、上架元数据规范，以及购买现号时的交接核对标准，帮助开发者在自主申请与购买老号间做出准确决策。

## 加拿大主体资质与账号类型的匹配条件

获取加拿大苹果开发者账号，个人号需要加拿大 residency 或公民身份及当地手机号；公司号则需要邓白氏编号。如果是购买现号，必须确认主体类型与自身业务是否匹配。个人号无法直接升级为公司号，若业务涉及企业级分发或需要展示公司名称，必须购买或申请公司号，切勿因价格差异购买错误类型导致后续无法上架特定应用。

## App 上架核心资料与元数据规范

上架应用需要准备隐私政策 URL，该链接必须支持 HTTPS 且可公开访问，不能是占位符。应用图标和截图需严格符合 iOS 尺寸规范，且截图中不得包含测试数据或空白界面。针对加拿大区，虽然苹果默认接受纯英文上架，但若目标市场包含魁北克省等法语区，提供准确的法语本地化描述和隐私政策是避免合规审查被拒的必要条件。

## 购买加拿大现号时的资料交接核对

购买老号时，交接核对决定了账号的可用性。必须验证 Apple ID 的恢复邮箱和绑定的加拿大手机号能否正常接收双重认证验证码。对于公司号，还需核对邓白氏编号的归属状态，确认该主体名称和地址在苹果后台与邓白氏数据库完全一致，且未处于被苹果封禁或拉黑的状态，否则即使账号交接成功，后续生成的证书也会受限。

## 证书与描述文件的权限边界

无论是新申请还是购买的账号，上架前均需生成证书和描述文件。如果购买的是带应用的老号，需确认原证书是否还能继续使用，或者是否需要重新生成。需要明确的是，如果原账号存在违规记录或被限制过，重新生成的证书同样会继承这些限制。下单前必须要求卖家提供后台证书状态的完整截图，确认没有未解决的违规警告。

## 自主申请与购买现号的决策标准

如果具备加拿大本地身份和邓白氏编号，自主申请成本最低且账号归属最清晰；如果缺乏加拿大主体资质，或需要立即上架且无法等待数周的审核与资料同步期，购买已验证的加拿大现号是更优解。决策的核心在于时间成本与资质门槛的权衡，若选择购买，必须严格执行上述资料交接核对，避免因账号主体问题导致应用被下架。

# Canada Apple Developer Account Application and App Listing Materials

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [Canada Apple Developer Account Application and App Listing Materials](https://faya101.com/en/blog/articles/canada-apple-developer-account-app-listing-materials-2.html)

Obtaining a Canada Apple Developer account and listing apps requires clear entity qualifications and material lists. This article outlines the requirements for individual and organization accounts, metadata specs, and handover verification for buying existing accounts, helping developers make accurate decisions.

## Matching Canadian Entity Qualifications with Account Types

To obtain a Canada Apple Developer account, individual accounts require Canadian residency or citizenship along with a local phone number; organization accounts need a D-U-N-S number. When buying an existing account, ensure the entity type matches your business needs. Individual accounts cannot be directly upgraded to organization accounts. If your business involves enterprise distribution or requires displaying a company name, you must buy or apply for an organization account, avoiding the wrong type due to price differences.

## Core App Store Listing Materials and Metadata Specs

Listing an app requires a privacy policy URL, which must support HTTPS and be publicly accessible, not a placeholder. App icons and screenshots must strictly meet iOS size specifications, and screenshots must not contain test data or blank interfaces. For the Canadian region, while Apple accepts pure English listings by default, providing accurate French localized descriptions and privacy policies is necessary to avoid compliance rejections if your target market includes French-speaking areas like Quebec.

## Verifying Handover Materials When Buying Existing Accounts

When buying an old account, handover verification determines its usability. You must verify whether the Apple ID recovery email and the bound Canadian phone number can normally receive two-factor authentication codes. For organization accounts, you also need to verify the ownership status of the D-U-N-S number, ensuring the entity name and address exactly match the D&B database and Apple backend, and that it is not banned or blacklisted by Apple, otherwise regenerated certificates will be restricted.

## Permission Boundaries for Certificates and Provisioning Profiles

Whether newly applied or bought, certificates and provisioning profiles must be generated before listing. If buying an old account with an existing app, confirm whether the original certificates are still valid or if regeneration is needed. Note that if the original account has violation records or restrictions, regenerated certificates will inherit these limitations. Before ordering, require the seller to provide complete screenshots of the backend certificate status to ensure there are no unresolved violation warnings.

## Decision Criteria: Self-Registration vs. Buying Existing Accounts

If you have a local Canadian identity and a D-U-N-S number, self-registration is the cheapest and offers the clearest account ownership. If you lack Canadian entity qualifications or need immediate listing without waiting weeks for review and material synchronization, buying a verified existing Canadian account is the better option. The core of the decision lies in weighing time costs against qualification thresholds. If choosing to buy, strictly execute the above material handover verification to avoid app removal due to entity issues.
