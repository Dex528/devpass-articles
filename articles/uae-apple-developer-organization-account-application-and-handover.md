## 邓白氏编码与商业执照的严格匹配规则

在阿联酋申请苹果开发者公司账号，最大的卡点在于邓白氏（D-U-N-S）编码与阿联酋当地商业执照（Trade License）的信息比对。苹果要求数据库中的公司英文名称、地址必须与执照上的注册信息完全一致，包括空格、缩写和标点。如果执照包含阿拉伯文，必须确保邓白氏登记的英文翻译与官方认可的标准译名一致。任何细微的格式差异都会导致验证直接驳回，且重新提交审核的周期不可控。

## 域名邮箱与本地实体验证的硬性条件

苹果对公司账号的实体验证要求申请者使用公司专属域名邮箱，且该域名必须有实际的 MX 解析记录以接收验证邮件。此外，验证环节通常需要拨打阿联酋本地电话或接收本地手机号的短信验证码。如果使用的虚拟号码或海外号码无法稳定接收苹果的系统呼叫，验证流程将陷入死循环。这是自主申请时极易被忽视的底层基础设施要求。

## 转向购买老号的决策条件与时机

当公司刚完成注册、邓白氏审核周期超出项目预期，或急需应用上架时，应考虑直接购买已验证的阿联酋公司老号。购买老号的核心价值在于跳过主体验证的等待期，直接获取已激活的开发者权限。判断是否购买的标准是：你的业务是否允许等待 2-4 周甚至更长的不可控审核期。如果不允许，且预算充足，购买带有完整主体验证状态的老号是唯一解。

## 购买阿联酋公司老号的核心交接清单

下单购买阿联酋公司老号前，必须核对四项关键信息：第一，原公司的 Trade License 是否在有效期内，且未处于注销或吊销状态；第二，账号后台绑定的邓白氏编码是否仍与当前执照匹配；第三，账号是否存在未处理的违规警告（Warning）或封禁记录；第四，确认 Account Holder（账户持有人）权限的交接方式，是彻底转移给买家的 Apple ID，还是仅提供共享登录，后者存在极高的被原号主找回的风险。

# UAE Apple Developer Organization Account Application Issues and Old Account Handover Checks

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [UAE Apple Developer Organization Account Application Issues and Old Account Handover Checks](https://faya101.com/en/blog/articles/uae-apple-developer-organization-account-application-and-handover.html)

Applying for a UAE Apple Developer Organization account often fails due to mismatches between the D-U-N-S number and the Trade License. This article analyzes verification bottlenecks in self-registration and defines the checklist for purchasing existing accounts, including license validity, permission handover, and renewal conditions, to help developers make accurate decisions.

## Strict Alignment Between D-U-N-S and Trade License

The primary bottleneck when applying for an Apple Developer Organization account in the UAE is the exact matching of the D-U-N-S number with the local Trade License. Apple requires the company's English name and address in the D-U-N-S database to perfectly match the registered information on the license, including spaces, abbreviations, and punctuation. If the license contains Arabic, the English translation in the D-U-N-S record must align with the officially recognized standard. Any minor formatting discrepancy will result in immediate rejection, and the resubmission cycle is unpredictable.

## Mandatory Domain Email and Local Entity Verification

Apple's entity verification for organization accounts mandates the use of a corporate domain email, which must have active MX records to receive verification emails. Additionally, the verification process often requires answering a call or receiving an SMS on a UAE local phone number. If virtual numbers or overseas numbers are used and fail to stably receive Apple's system calls, the verification process will deadlock. This underlying infrastructure requirement is frequently overlooked during self-registration.

## Decision Criteria for Switching to an Existing Account

You should consider purchasing an already verified UAE organization old account when your company is newly registered, the D-U-N-S review cycle exceeds project expectations, or you urgently need to launch your app. The core value of buying an old account is bypassing the entity verification waiting period to directly access activated developer privileges. The decision standard is whether your business can tolerate a 2-to-4-week or longer uncontrollable review period. If not, and the budget allows, purchasing an old account with a fully verified entity status is the only viable solution.

## Core Handover Checklist for Purchased UAE Organization Accounts

Before placing an order for a UAE organization old account, four critical items must be verified: First, check if the original company's Trade License is valid and not revoked or deregistered. Second, confirm whether the D-U-N-S number bound in the account backend still matches the current license. Third, ensure there are no unresolved warnings or suspension records in the account. Fourth, clarify the handover method for the Account Holder role—whether it is completely transferred to the buyer's Apple ID or if only shared login credentials are provided, the latter carrying a high risk of recovery by the original owner.

## Renewal Logic and Entity Existence Risks Post-Acquisition

After taking over an old account, ensure the bound payment method, such as a locally issued UAE credit card, is logically consistent with the entity. The more critical risk lies in entity existence: Apple may trigger a re-verification of entity information during annual account renewals or certificate regeneration. If the original seller's Trade License has been deregistered or the D-U-N-S number is invalid, the account will fail verification, leading to certificate update failures or account suspension. Therefore, purchasing an old account essentially means buying the continuity of the original entity's qualifications, requiring confirmation of the original entity's long-term existence or a viable change plan.
