## 邓白氏编码与 ABN/ACN 的严格映射

澳大利亚企业（Organization）申请苹果开发者账号，必须提供邓白氏编码（D-U-N-S Number）。该编码在邓白氏系统中的企业名称、物理地址必须与澳大利亚商业编号（ABN）或公司编号（ACN）的注册信息完全一致。任何缩写、后缀（如 Pty Ltd 与 Pty. Ltd.）或地址格式的差异，都会导致苹果系统验证失败。申请前需确保邓白氏数据库中的信息已更新至最新状态。

## 实体类型选择与 ASIC 注册匹配

在苹果开发者注册流程中，实体类型（Entity Type）的选择是常见驳回点。澳大利亚企业必须根据澳大利亚证券和投资委员会（ASIC）的注册类型进行选择。例如，若注册为 proprietary limited company，应选择「Corporation」。若误选为「Government Organization」或「Non-Profit」，苹果审核团队在比对公开企业记录时会直接拒绝。选择前需核对 ASIC 出具的官方注册文件。

## 域名邮箱与企业官网的硬性验证

苹果对公司账号的合法性审查包含数字资产验证。注册使用的 Apple ID 必须绑定企业专属域名邮箱（如 name@company.com.au），严禁使用 Gmail、Outlook 等公共邮箱。同时，企业必须拥有可公开访问的官方网站，且网站内容需与申请业务相关。苹果可能会通过 WHOIS 记录核查域名所有权，若域名注册人与申请企业名称不符，或网站无法访问，申请将被终止。

## 申请周期卡点与现号取舍标准

自主申请澳大利亚苹果开发者公司账号的周期较长。邓白氏编码的新申请或信息更新通常需要数周，苹果的人工审核在材料不齐时也会反复要求补充。如果企业业务急需上架 App Store，或者目前仅有公共邮箱而无独立企业官网，自主注册的驳回风险极高。此时，直接购买已完成邓白氏验证、域名绑定且状态正常的澳大利亚苹果开发者公司老号，是更稳妥的替代方案。下单前需核对老号的实体类型与当前业务是否兼容。

# Australian Company Apple Developer Account Application: D-U-N-S and Entity Type Verification

This file mirrors the site article. Official page: [Australian Company Apple Developer Account Application: D-U-N-S and Entity Type Verification](https://faya101.com/en/blog/articles/australian-company-apple-developer-account-application.html)

Applying for an Australian company Apple Developer account requires strict alignment of D-U-N-S numbers with ABN/ACN. This guide covers entity type selection, domain email verification, and bottlenecks to help decide between self-registration and buying an existing account.

## Strict Mapping of D-U-N-S with ABN/ACN

Australian organizations applying for an Apple Developer account must provide a D-U-N-S Number. The legal entity name and physical address in the D-U-N-S database must exactly match the Australian Business Number (ABN) or Australian Company Number (ACN) registration details. Any discrepancies in abbreviations, suffixes (e.g., Pty Ltd vs. Pty. Ltd.), or address formats will cause Apple's verification to fail. Ensure the D-U-N-S records are fully updated before initiating the application.

## Entity Type Selection and ASIC Compliance

Selecting the correct Entity Type during the Apple Developer enrollment is a frequent cause of rejection. Australian companies must align their selection with their registration type under the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC). For instance, a proprietary limited company must select Corporation. Choosing Government Organization or Non-Profit by mistake will lead to immediate rejection when Apple cross-references public corporate records. Always verify against official ASIC documents before proceeding.

## Mandatory Domain Email and Website Verification

Apple's legitimacy check for organization accounts includes digital asset verification. The Apple ID used for enrollment must be associated with a corporate domain email (e.g., name@company.com.au); public email services like Gmail or Outlook are strictly prohibited. Additionally, the company must have a publicly accessible website relevant to the business. Apple may verify domain ownership via WHOIS records; if the domain registrant does not match the applying entity or the website is inaccessible, the application will be terminated.

## Application Bottlenecks and Decision Criteria for Existing Accounts

Self-registering an Australian Apple Developer organization account involves a lengthy timeline. Applying for or updating a D-U-N-S number can take several weeks, and Apple's manual review may repeatedly request supplementary documents. If your business urgently needs to publish apps on the App Store, or if you currently lack an independent corporate website and rely solely on public emails, the risk of self-registration rejection is high. In such cases, purchasing an existing, fully verified Australian Apple Developer company account is a more reliable alternative. Before ordering, verify that the existing account's entity type is compatible with your current business needs.
